Project: Flourish

Summary

Flourish is the result of a more ambitious university project executed during the COVID-19 pandemic 2021. The concept revolves around positive reinforcement for healthy habits, both physically and mentally, as well as breaking the trend of social isolation that plagued many young, single-person households around Sweden. The concept allows for customisation based on what type of tasks the user needs support with, as well as a more dynamic day-to-day customisation depending on whether the user is having a good or bad day, which affects the ambition and energy demand levels of the tasks.

Goal

Flourish was designed with the intended purpose to help young adults living alone with declining mental health, through the Covid-19 pandemic. The focus is to help with daily tasks and to guide people back into healthy habits.
