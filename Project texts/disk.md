### Summary



DISK.SU.SE is the official site of the student union of Stockholm University Computer science department. The site was abruptly taken down during my time as communication manager. A new website had to be constructed from scratch, as soon as possible. After two weeks of intense work, I had successfully created a viable foundation for DISK's new website.



I continued to improve the website throughout my engagement within the union board, but it was later handed over to the new communication manager to administer. The screen shots provided on this page reflects the state of the website as I handed it over.



### Goal



The purpose of the page is to act as an information channel towards students of Stockholms Universitet, DSV. This included information about the union itself, a news feed about upcoming events and other updates, as well as general guidance for the students of the campus.







### Background



This project was initiated in 2019, when the website of DISK (the student union at DSV) abruptly got taken down for security reasons.

As the current communication manager within the student union board, I took the responsibility of setting up a new one.

In short, I was the project owner, Product owner, UX/UI designer, writer, developer, and even user, of this project,

and within two weeks we had a functional website in Swedish representing a minimum viable product. After the first launch, the page was translated into English and underwent continuous improvements for a better user experience and increased clarity.​



The site is powered by WordPress, using the theme Divi, a highly functional tool with plenty of customization options and modules.

A lot of the content from the old page, such as images and plain texts, could be salvaged from the old page.

Although, the design and website structured was lost as it was hosted by the university and not the student union.





### Design process



Since this was a project with a incredibly tight deadline, this affected the depth of the design process.

Most of the work was done by simply finding a WordPress theme,

and designing directly within WordPress instead of proper prototyping.

The menu structure of the page was visualized in FigJam to effectively explore different grouping and

clustering alternatives of pages and content.



The colour scheme of the page was based on the visual identity documentation established for the student union.

In retrospect, the colour scheme should definitely have been adjusted, as some defined combinations do not meet accessibility guidelines. However, at the time of this project, this was knowledge I did not yet have.
